How to play

Each player starts with six cards. Whoever holds the lowest trump attacks first. Once the deck runs out, empty your hand to win.

Attack. Lead with any card. To add another, match the rank of any card on the table. You can attack with up to six cards per round, capped at the number of cards the defender held at the start of the round.

Defend. Beat an attack with a higher card of the same suit, or use a trump to beat any non-trump. Only a higher trump beats a trump. Choose Pick up to take all the cards on the table.

End the round. Once all attacks are beaten, the attacker can choose Pass to discard the cards on the table. Both players draw back up to six while cards remain in the deck. The defender attacks next if they beat every card. If they picked up, the same attacker leads again.
